AurelionPack v4.1.0 by PAXANDDOS

This mod pack includes a decent amount of mods. It is preconfigured with optimal settings, keeping the horror vibe of the game.
The main goal is to be as performant as possible by including best-in-class performance mods, most of the mods are thoughtfully configured to work efficiently.

- AurelionPack Performance
- AurelionPack Host
